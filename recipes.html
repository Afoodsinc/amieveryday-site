Black bean rice

ami products in this recipe

ami Black Beans

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 can ami Black Beans
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • Lime and cilantro to serve

Method

  1. Warm the beans with the onion in a saucepan.
  2. Fold in the cooked rice and heat through.
  3. Finish with lime and cilantro.
Prep: 25 min · Serves 4

Pantry tomato pasta

ami products in this recipe

ami Tomato Pasteami Whole Peeled Tomatoesami Tuna

Ingredients

  • 400 g pasta
  • 2 tbsp ami Tomato Paste
  • 1 can ami Whole Peeled Tomatoes
  • 1 can ami Tuna Chunks in Oil
  • Garlic and herbs

Method

  1. Cook the pasta until al dente.
  2. Cook the tomato paste and garlic for one minute, then add the tomatoes.
  3. Simmer for 10 minutes, fold in the tuna and toss with the pasta.
Prep: 15 min · Serves 4

Three-bean corn salad

ami products in this recipe

ami Sweet Cornami Pinto Beansami Red Kidney Beans

Ingredients

  • 1 can ami Sweet Corn
  • 1 can ami Pinto Beans
  • 1 can ami Red Kidney Beans
  • Bell pepper, lime and cilantro

Method

  1. Drain and rinse the beans and corn.
  2. Combine with the chopped pepper.
  3. Dress with lime, season and finish with cilantro.
Prep: 5 min · Serves 4

Mango and peach smoothie

ami products in this recipe

ami Mango Fruit Drinkami Sliced Peaches

Ingredients

  • 1 cup ami Mango Fruit Drink
  • ½ can ami Sliced Peaches, drained
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • 1 cup ice

Method

  1. Add everything to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and thick.
  3. Serve right away.
Prep: 30 min · Serves 4

Vegetable noodle soup

ami products in this recipe

ami Mixed Vegetablesami Chopped Tomatoesami Instant Noodles

Ingredients

  • 1 can ami Mixed Vegetables
  • 1 can ami Chopped Tomatoes
  • 1 pack ami Instant Noodles, Chicken
  • Onion, garlic and water

Method

  1. Soften the onion and garlic in a pot.
  2. Add the tomatoes and water; simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the vegetables and noodles, then cook until the noodles are tender.
Prep: 15 min · Serves 4

Garlic-soy mushrooms

ami products in this recipe

ami Whole Button Mushroomsami Dark Soy Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 cans ami Whole Button Mushrooms, drained
  • 1 tbsp ami Dark Soy Sauce
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • Scallions and black pepper

Method

  1. Pat the mushrooms dry and sear them in a hot pan.
  2. Add the garlic and cook for one minute.
  3. Add the soy sauce, toss until glossy and finish with scallions.
