Private label is taking a bigger share of the world’s basket.

The story is no longer a single year of growth. Private-label share of global consumer spending rose from 15% in 2009 to 22% in 2024. Across mature markets, the opportunity is already measured in hundreds of billions—and the loyalty is lasting beyond inflation.
