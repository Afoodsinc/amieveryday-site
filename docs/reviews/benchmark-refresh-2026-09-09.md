# ami anytime benchmark refresh — 2026-09-09

## Purpose

Translate current food-brand patterns into an ami-native website improvement without copying another brand, changing the approved navy/green identity, or deciding the seven owner-gated items in `HANDOFF.md` section 4.

## Current official references reviewed

- [Essential Everyday](https://www.essentialeveryday.com/) — direct shopper path from promise to Products, Recipes, and Store Locator; featured products before catalogue breadth.
- [ALDI Brands](https://www.aldi.us/store/aldi/pages/aldi-brands) and [ALDI food philosophy](https://corporate.aldi.us/about-us/our-food-philosophy) — category-led browsing and specific evidence beside trust claims.
- [Trader Joe's products](https://www.traderjoes.com/home/products/category/for-the-pantry-137) and [recipes](https://www.traderjoes.com/home/recipes) — useful intent filters and precise catalogue-availability language.
- [365 by Whole Foods Market](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/departments/365-products) — one masterbrand across familiar grocery departments with a strong product-first visual system.
- [Goya English](https://www.goya.com/en/products/) and [Goya Spanish](https://www.goya.com/es/products/) — dedicated language routes and recipe-to-product connections.
- [Food Club](https://www.foodclub.com/products) and [Best Choice](https://bestchoicebrand.com/product/) — product name, size, catalogue search, recipes, and where-to-buy as practical shopper utilities.
- [Graza](https://mygraza.com/), [Fishwife](https://eatfishwife.com/), and [Fly By Jing](https://flybyjing.com/) — modern food-brand confidence through bold product staging, editorial scale, and a small number of clear actions.

## Chosen ami direction

**Modern pantry:** generous white space, navy editorial typography, green utility cues, real ami packshots on softly tinted product stages, rounded cards, and aisle colors used as navigation rather than decoration.

The experience should move a visitor through four clear jobs:

1. understand what ami is;
2. find a product;
3. cook with it;
4. see launch markets or enter the retail-partner path.

## Patterns applied

- A dedicated shopper/retailer pathway on the homepage.
- A real bilingual catalogue search by product name, category, and size, with diacritic-insensitive matching and compatibility with the canned-goods filters.
- Stronger product cards, category stages, recipe cards, page introductions, and responsive hierarchy inside the existing identity.
- Evidence-safe About and product-support language in place of unverified producer, price, and return-policy statements on those pages.
- Product-to-recipe connection on the tomato-paste detail page.
- Honest controls: the false PDP tab links, dead social links, and placeholder legal links are no longer presented as clickable actions.
- Accessible language switching, active-page semantics, search focus, and Escape-to-close mobile navigation.

## Deliberately not copied

- Another brand's colors, typography, packaging, or voice.
- Ecommerce carts, prices, reviews, local inventory, or delivery features without a retailer data feed.
- National-brand comparisons, savings percentages, quality superiority, certifications, testing, sourcing, or sustainability claims without ami evidence.
- Large recipe taxonomies for a six-recipe collection.
- A flat 2,000-item catalogue pattern that makes breadth harder to browse.

## Owner-gated items preserved

No decision was made on the homepage hero copy, 29-versus-149 catalogue structure, forms provider, legal-page text, catalogue truth, where-to-buy prelaunch wording, or the single partner sentence. Those remain in `HANDOFF.md` section 4.

## Acceptance standard

- English and Spanish remain paired with reciprocal canonical and hreflang links.
- All primary pages retain one H1 and parseable JSON-LD.
- Every local image and internal destination exists.
- Product search works in English and Spanish, clears cleanly, combines with category filters, and leaves the full catalogue visible when JavaScript is unavailable.
- Mobile aisle navigation remains horizontally contained; the page has no document-level horizontal overflow.
- No production `href="#"` remains.
- CNAME, hosting, DNS, accounts, and third-party services remain untouched.
